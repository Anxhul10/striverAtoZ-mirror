# A2Z Sheet

**Completely free and open source.**

I really liked the old Striver A2Z website, especially its blogs and articles. I found them helpful while learning DSA, and I wanted to bring back that kind of experience for my own practice.

So, I built this tracker for personal use. I decided to make it open source because I thought others might feel the same way. If you miss that experience too, feel free to use it!

This is an independent community project, not the official Take U Forward website.

## What can you do with it?

- Follow DSA topics and subtopics.
- Mark problems as completed.
- Add your own notes.
- Keep problems for revision.
- Export and import your progress as a JSON file.
- Read and contribute articles.

> **Articles are still being added, and you can help!** If you have a different way of understanding a problem, an explanation that helped you, or something worth sharing, feel free to contribute.

## Save and restore your progress

Your progress is saved automatically in your browser.

### Save a backup

1. Open the tracker.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Keep the downloaded `striver-a2z-progress.json` file somewhere safe.

### Load a saved backup

1. Open the tracker.
2. Click **Import**.
3. Select your saved `striver-a2z-progress.json` file.

Importing a file replaces the progress currently saved in that browser. Export your current progress first if you want to keep a backup.

## Contribute an article

Example pull request: [#1](https://github.com/Anxhul10/striverAtoZ-mirror/pull/1)

For the full instructions, see the [article contribution guide](contribute.md).

**A small request for contributors:** Please don't just copy and paste Striver's articles here. The goal is to share your own understanding. Explain the approach in your own words, share your experience, mention mistakes you made, or add an alternative explanation that might help someone else.

Even a simple explanation can make a difficult problem easier for another learner.
